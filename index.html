About Me

Hi! I recently started a Masters of Engineering program at the University of California, Irvine with a focus in Electrical Engineering and concentration in Communications, Circuits, and Systems. I previously worked at Industrial Light & Magic as a technical assistant. At ILM, I contributed to a large-scale backend system to support production, including refactoring a resource tracking project used by rendering managers globally, resolving technical errors on the render farm, and maintaining workflow consistency across teams.

Since I've started UCI, I have taken numerous IC design, Semicon. Physics, and MEMS Design courses (academic projects are highlighted in the projects section). I'm currently interested in hardware, analog, and validation engineering roles.

I graduated from University of California, Santa Cruz in 2022. During my time there, I participated in a few clubs such as the National Society of Black Engineers(NSBE) and CruzHacks, a non-profit hackathon held each year, where I assissted the sponsorship team. I also joined the NueroTech team. We created a headset that won first place in our division's competition.
